Post-Budget LTCG Tax Rules for Mutual Funds

Under the latest tax regulations in India, equity mutual fund capital gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakhs per financial year are subject to a flat 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax. Our tool accurately calculates your taxable gains above this exemption, giving you your exact net in-hand wealth corpus.

Understanding Step-Up SIP (The Compounding Accelerator)

A standard SIP keeps your monthly contribution constant throughout your tenure. However, as your career progresses and income rises, increasing your SIP amount annually by 10% or 15% (known as a Step-Up SIP) exponentially boosts your final wealth corpus without placing immediate financial strain on your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How is the LTCG tax calculated in this tool?
The calculator subtracts your total principal invested from the final maturity corpus to compute gross returns. An exemption of ₹1,25,000 is deducted from the gains, and a 12.5% tax is applied to the remaining taxable profit.

2. What is a Step-Up SIP and why should I use it?
A Step-Up SIP automatically raises your monthly mutual fund investment by a chosen percentage every year, mirroring your salary hikes and dramatically accelerating compounding.

3. What benchmark return rates should I choose?
Historically over 10-15 year horizons, Large Cap index funds yield around 12%, Mid Caps around 14%, and Small Cap funds range around 16% CAGR.
